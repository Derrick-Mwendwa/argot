# Explanation

Background on why Argot is shaped the way it is. Useful when you want to understand the design rather
than accomplish a task.

- [Two styles, one engine](./two-styles) — why the delegate and annotation styles cannot drift apart.
- [Why there is no runtime reflection](./no-reflection) — what that buys you, and what it costs.
- [How optionality is inferred](./optionality) — what the processor can and cannot see about your
  constructor.
- [What counts as a breaking change](./breaking-changes) — the three surfaces a release can break.

These pages assume you have written a command line with Argot already. If you have not, the
[tutorial](../tutorial/your-first-cli) comes first.
